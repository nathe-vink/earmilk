**Proves:** the sleeve is two pieces of board that come off and go on; the cabinet underneath is birch and outlives its skin. This is the exploded view the Decisions section allows: bare birch body and gable are expected here and nowhere else.

## Frames
- **a. Lid off.** The lid lifted clear above the gable. The bare birch gable block with the carved bowl shows; the tube is still on the body.
- **b. Tube sliding.** The old tube lifted partway up the body and off over the gable, the bare birch body with its drivers showing below the tube's bottom edge.
- **c. New flavor on.** Fully sleeved in the new flavor, tube and lid locked at the back seam.

## Flavors
- From **Whole** to **Oat** (a choice, to match shot 02; any pair of flavors is valid).

## Scene
- One speaker, a plain room or a flat ground. The same camera in all three frames.
- Hands are optional; if shown, they must not hide the lock or the seam.

## Camera
- Three-quarter view, the full speaker and the lifted parts in frame, camera at about 1.2 m.

## Must show
- The sleeve's two pieces as thin board (1.2 to 1.5 mm): the tube open top and bottom, the lid with its die-cut mouth.
- Bare birch in a and b: body, baffle with the drivers mounted, the carved gable with the bowl.
- In c, no birch except the back window if the back is visible.

## Must not
- Anything else in the list in `prompts/README.md`.
- Show any fastener, tool, hinge or mechanism beyond the two pieces and the back seam.
