# Shot 05 · Five flavors lineup · v1

v1: first brief, from the README shot list.

**Proves:** flavor is the colorway only. Five identical objects, five skins.

## Scene
- Five speakers in flavor order, left to right: **Whole, 2%, Skim, Chocolate, Oat**.
- Flat ground, #F8F7F4, no horizon line visible or a very soft one. Equal spacing between all five.
- Soft, even studio light.

## Camera
- Straight on or a very slight three-quarter, camera at about half the speaker's height (`z` ≈ 530 mm).
- Long lens so all five are the same size and the row stays parallel to the frame.

## Must show
- Five identical silhouettes and driver layouts; only board colour, print colour and throat colour change.
- Each wordmark in its flavor's print colour; Chocolate and Oat on their whole-board colours.
- The bowl mouth on each, with each flavor's throat gradient where it is visible.

## Must not
- Anything in the list in `prompts/README.md`.
- Vary the size, spacing or pose between the five.

## Text-to-image form
Constant block, then: Five of them in a row on a flat pale off-white ground, equally spaced, straight on, soft even studio light, long lens. Left to right: white board with a red wordmark; white board with a blue wordmark; white board with a light-blue wordmark; a dark brown board with a cream wordmark; an unbleached oat-coloured board with a dark brown wordmark. The speakers are otherwise identical. Photoreal, matte board, no grille.

- A viewer sees one product in five colorways, not five products.
- Hex values read true against `spec/colorways.json` under the neutral light.
