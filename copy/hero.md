## Sleeves

- The cabinet is birch: a body and a carved gable. The sleeve is two pieces of heavy printed board: a tube that slides up over the body, and a lid, die-cut at the mouth, that drops over the gable. Both lock at the back seam. The wordmark sits in a band at the foot; on the white cartons the lid takes the flavor’s color. *(last sentence added on the canvas 2026-10-02)*
- Rooms change. Swap the sleeve in ten minutes and the speaker changes with them. Any flavor, shipped flat.
- Price line: Sleeves, [SLEEVE PRICE] a set

## Section (the bowl)

- The tweeter faces forward from the throat of a bowl carved into the gable; the mouth is an ellipse on the slope. The bowl is the waveguide: it flares smoothly, so there are no edges to ring, and its lips set the pattern.
- As drawn, the lower lip holds the pattern to about 12° down and the upper lip to about 37° up, aimed at a seated listener. Set 170 mm back, the dome lands roughly where its sound lines up with the mid. A deeper bowl narrows the pattern; a wider mouth opens it.
- Caption: Side section at the centerline. Front is left.
- Callouts: tweeter set back 170 mm · mouth 118 mm · tweeter · 6.5 in mid · fin · carved birch gable · sleeve · lid
